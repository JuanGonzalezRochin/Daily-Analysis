Reference · decision rules

No action / hold
Within target, or a recent test hasn't accumulated enough data. State the reason briefly and keep collecting. Don't make unnecessary changes.
Action required
A decision threshold is reached, performance is below target, or behaviour data clearly supports a test. One specific change, why, and what you'll measure.
Technical issue
Tracking, forms, automations, links, events, redirects, calendars or data flow are broken. Diagnose and fix first — flag immediately, don't wait for a visitor threshold.
Testing rule
Change one meaningful variable at a time so we can understand what caused the result. Benchmarks: landing page 15%+, booking 5–10%, sales-page decision point at 30 new visitors.
